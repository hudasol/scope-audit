# Attribution for the demo images

These 60 reduced images (longest side 640 pixels) and the working set feature table come from the Caltech Camera Traps data set, hosted by LILA BC (https://lila.science/datasets/caltech-camera-traps).

- Data set: Caltech Camera Traps. Citation: Sara Beery, Grant Van Horn and Pietro Perona, "Recognition in Terra Incognita", ECCV 2018.
- Licence: Community Data License Agreement, permissive variant.
- Images were collected in the Southwestern United States with help from the National Park Service and the United States Geological Survey. Rights holders are recorded in the source metadata (for example Erin Boydston).
- Label: BORROWED. These images are not from the UAE. They are used only to demonstrate the software. Species labels shown in the demo are benchmark labels standing in for an expert decision.
