# Pitch 🌷

## One sentence

Scope Audit tells a wildlife programme how many animals its detector hid inside the images it called empty, and gives the programme a trail from machine suggestion to approved record.

## Problem

Detectors remove most empty images, which saves months of review. Nobody knows what else they removed. A programme that cannot state its loss cannot defend its data.

## Solution

Review a small, well chosen sample of the images the detector called empty. Choose the sample so that images the detector was nearly sure about are weighted by risk. Estimate the loss with an interval, state honestly when reviewers cannot decide, and export only what a person approved.

## Evidence

Every figure comes from results generated by the code, never typed by hand. The comparison of sampling designs, the calibration of the intervals and the misses against our written predictions are in results/RESULTS.md. The data are labelled borrowed or synthetic because we used no data from the Emirates.

## Honest limits

The benchmark is from another country and was seen by the detector during training. The pilot exists to replace borrowed evidence with local evidence.

## Ask

A ninety day pilot with Ministry camera data and two reviewers.
