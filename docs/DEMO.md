# Demo script 🌿

Launch with `streamlit run app/streamlit_app.py`. The banner states that the data are borrowed with synthetic coordinates.

1. Ingest and integrity. Sixty demo images are hashed and linked to the synthetic registry. Show the counts, the flags and the wording used for gaps: operation could not be established, never a claim of malfunction.
2. Audit. Move the budget slider and compare the simple random design with the confidence strata design. Show the estimate, the interval, the hidden events found per hundred reviews, and raise the unresolved share to show the bounds appear instead of a guess.
3. Review, approve and export. As reviewer, record a species for one event. Try to export and see it refused. Switch to approver and approve. Switch to administrator and export. Open the downloaded package and show the three tables and the audit package. Show the hash chain check on the history.

Say plainly at each step what is borrowed, what is synthetic, and what a real pilot replaces.
